How the markers were placed

Projects at existing buildings sit on that building. Where no site has been announced, the marker sits in the named community and is flagged "approx." in the list. For those, the district is a best guess.

  • Three Rivers Health Campus: land is off Route 4 in Three Rivers; exact parcel not mapped.
  • New Stratford Intermediate: in Mermaid–Stratford (District 5); the marker shows the district, not the exact site.
  • New West Royalty school: no site announced. The marker falls in Charlottetown–Winsloe, but the school could be in Charlottetown–West Royalty.
  • Green Gables Elementary: treated as the new East Royalty school beside L.M. Montgomery Elementary. Not confirmed.
  • Summerside's Parkside replacement: no site announced.
